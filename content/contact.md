### If You Need Immediate Help

For a medical emergency, please dial 911 in the US.  If you are in crisis or suicidal, please call the Lifeline at 988.  For referrals for treatment for substance abuse (including alcohol) or other mental health issues, call the free SAMSHA Helpline at 1-800-662-4357.
